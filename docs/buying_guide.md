# Property Buying Checklist & Guidelines

1. Property Title Verification:
   Always verify the original title deed, mutation records, and non-encumbrance certificate before signing any real estate agreement.

2. Property Valuation:
   Evaluate market value based on living area (sq ft), neighborhood trends, overall quality rating, and building age.

3. Taxes & Legal Documentation:
   Ensure all local municipal taxes, utility bills, and transfer duties are fully cleared by the seller.
